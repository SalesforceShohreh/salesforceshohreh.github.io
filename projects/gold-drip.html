Business Context

Company Overview | Gold Drip

A fictional wellness business scenario created to demonstrate how Salesforce can support growth, service delivery, customer relationship management, and process structure.

01

Where the Company Started

Gold Drip began as a wellness-focused business offering physical products through an e-commerce storefront. As operations expanded, customer information and follow-up activities required a more structured system than standalone online transactions could provide.

02

Where the Company Is Going

The business vision expands beyond products into a broader wellness ecosystem that includes consultations, educational content, fitness resources, wellness programs, and more personalized customer experiences.

03

Why Salesforce Was Implemented

This direction requires centralized customer data, organized sales and service processes, better follow-up visibility, reporting, automation, and secure access to business information.

Implementation Goal

I independently implemented a Salesforce CRM solution to create a structured operational foundation for Gold Drip’s evolving business model and to demonstrate end-to-end Salesforce Administrator capabilities in a realistic business scenario.

Project Summary

Project Overview

Gold Drip is an independent Salesforce CRM implementation project I initiated alongside my collaborative ReRun Athletics capstone. I created this additional project to gain hands-on experience completing every stage of a Salesforce implementation independently.

I personally designed, configured, tested, and documented the Salesforce solution, including CRM administration, custom objects, data imports, sales processes, Flow automation, Case Management, Prompt Builder, reporting, dashboards, and role-based security.

The implementation uses fictional customer records and demonstration datasets. All 20 requirements in my project rubric tracker are marked complete, with the final recorded walkthrough remaining.

Project Ownership
